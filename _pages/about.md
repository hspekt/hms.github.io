I am a scientist using chemistry, mass spectrometry and computation to satisfy my curiosity about biology. Towards this end, I co-founded Parallel Squared Technology Institute [(PTI)](https://parallelsq.org/team) to advance protein measurement in human health and disease. Previously, I was a post-doctoral researcher and graduate student in the [Slavov Laboratory](https://slavovlab.net) at Northeastern University.
